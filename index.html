Nigeria · Social Justice, partnerships, programmes

I help good organisations get the support they need to keep going.

Alphonse is a Philosophy graduate building a career in grants management, strategic partnerships and resource mobilisation for social impact. He focuses on strengthening organisational systems, coordinating partnerships and supporting the processes that turn ideas into effective programmes. His goal is to contribute to sustainable development by helping mission-driven organisations create meaningful, measurable and lasting impact.
