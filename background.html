Background

Where I studied, and what I keep thinking about.

What I think about

How technology changes the way people live together. I studied Heidegger's worries about technology in philosophy class, and I still ask the same thing about every new tool: what is it quietly doing to us?

I'm still working out which of these becomes the main road and which stays a side path. For now I'm enjoying the walk.
